L'Ouvroir is participating in the "Data Commons and AI" Forum, which will be held at the INRS.

Generative AI is profoundly transforming access to—and the governance of—open cultural data. Whether originating from libraries, archives, museums, or digital commons like Wikipedia, this data is now being used intensively by AI systems. How can institutions and communities respond? This is the core question addressed by the "Data Commons and AI" Forum, organized by a Franco-Quebecois consortium. As part of this initiative, we are hosting a free online webinar:

Wednesday, October 28, 2026
9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. (Montreal time)

Panel 1 | Governance and Public Policy (in French)

What role should data commons play in the public governance of AI, as we approach the 2027 Geneva Summit? Discussions will cover the integration of commons into government strategies, the public sector’s role in the governance of "agentic" protocols, and open-source filtering solutions.

Panel 2 | Mapping Collective Initiatives (in English)

An overview of international coalitions mobilizing to strengthen data commons, featuring Fenwick McKelvey (Concordia University, Machine Agencies), Lorena Dianel Aldana Aldana (Europeana), E.M. Lewis-Jong (Mozilla Data Collective), and Maurice Jones (ArtIA).
