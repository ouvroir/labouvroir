## "Lunch Talks" on Digital Ecological Transition

Starting in November, Ouvroir offers its series of **"lunch talks"** held on the **second Thursday of each month, from 11:30 AM to 12:30 PM**. 

This year's theme is **digital ecological transition in museums and art history**. While digital tools have undoubtedly democratized access to collections, they rely on heavy material and energy infrastructure, marked by planned obsolescence and opaque dependencies. Rather than opposing these two realities, we propose exploring what a sober, chosen, and thoughtful digital approach can bring to our research and dissemination practices.

This cycle of lunch talks invites us to rethink our tools and methods by exploring lightweight workflows, the use of low-tech tools like Reframe or CollectionBuilder, rigorous evaluation and best practices for digital work in museums, as well as the exploration of frugal AI capable of running on its own machine. Guests (researchers, heritage professionals, and digital practitioners) will lead these exchanges around several axes. We look forward to revealing their names and backgrounds with each announcement. These sessions are designed as open spaces for everyone; come with your questions, projects, doubts, and ideas to build this approach together.

Whether you are starting from scratch or already have a project in mind, come with your ideas: the Ouvroir team will guide you step by step. The next date to remember is **December 10, 2026**. Do not hesitate to ask for more information!

## Developing a Critical Eye to Shape Tomorrow’s Discipline

In **February**, Kristine Tanton proposes a workshop dedicated to discussion with **master’s and doctoral students** regarding training in art history. 

This workshop continues two discussions held at Ouvroir during the 2024–2025 academic year, focused on art history training, particularly on what graduate programs in art history offer students. This third meeting proposes shifting the perspective: from what programs offer to what training enables students to do, think, and question.

## The Digital Clinic: Your Custom Appointment

Do you have a project, a question, or a technical blockage related to digital humanities? Our team is here to support you. The **digital clinic** is available **by appointment**: email us at ouvroir@umontreal.ca, and we will schedule a meeting tailored to your needs.
